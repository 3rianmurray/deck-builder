Privacy Policy

Shufflevale · Last updated October 18, 2026

Shufflevale is a card-deck app that keeps everything on your iPhone. In short: we don't collect any of your data.

What stays on your phone

The decks you make, the pictures you add to them (from your camera, photo library or files), your sessions, tables and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

Shufflevale asks for camera access only so you can photograph or scan your cards, and for photo library access only so you can choose card art. Those pictures are saved inside the app on your phone and go nowhere else.

Sharing decks

If you choose to share or back up a deck, Shufflevale makes a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there (Files, AirDrop, Messages and so on) is up to you. We never see it.

No accounts, tracking or ads

There are no accounts or sign-ins. Shufflevale has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't share or sell any information.

App updates

To deliver bug fixes and improvements, the app checks for updates from Expo's update service. This request contains only technical details needed to deliver the right update (such as the app's version and platform, and a random identifier made for this install that isn't linked to you), not your decks or any personal information.

Children

Shufflevale doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
