Getting started

A short guided tour starts the first time you open the app. To see it again, open the menu (☰) on the home screen and choose Help, then Replay the Tutorial. Help also lists every gesture.

Common questions

Where are my decks stored? Only on your iPhone, and they're included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup. With Shufflevale Pro you can also save them to a file: Back up all decks in the home screen's menu, or share a single deck from its ••• menu.

What does Shufflevale Pro add? The free version holds up to 60 of your own cards (the built-in Playing Cards deck doesn't count). Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the limit and adds deck files, for sharing decks with friends, backing them up and moving them to a new phone.

I bought Pro but it's locked. Open the menu (☰), choose Shufflevale Pro, then Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

I deleted the Solitaire table or the Playing Cards deck. Open the menu (☰) and choose Restore examples.

How do I bring in a sheet of many cards at once? In a deck, tap + Add and choose Import Sprite Sheet. Shufflevale works out the grid for you.
