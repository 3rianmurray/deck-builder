## Description (4000 max)
Shufflevale turns the cards you have into decks you can play on your phone: board game cards, homemade games, prototypes, flash cards, tarot, anything with a front and a back.

MAKE A DECK IN MINUTES
• Scan cards with the camera. Shufflevale finds each card's edges and straightens it for you.
• Import pictures from Photos or Files, or cut a whole sprite sheet into cards at once. It works out the grid for you, and you choose which cards to keep.
• Card size is worked out from your art, or choose Standard, US Mini, or a custom size in inches or millimeters.
• Set a card back for the whole deck, or give single cards their own back for two-sided cards.
• Set how many copies of each card, how round the corners are, and reorder cards by dragging.

FLIP THROUGH A DECK
Tap to turn the next card over onto the discard pile, with a satisfying riffle shuffle when it's time to start again. Search the deck, set cards aside, and pick up where you left off.

PLAY SEVERAL DECKS AT ONCE
Put decks together in a session and swipe between them, so a game's event, item and encounter decks are all one swipe away.

LAY IT ALL OUT ON A TABLE
Open a big table and bring on any of your decks, or just the cards you choose.
• Drag cards anywhere, flip them, turn them, and stack them. Hold a stack to move the whole thing.
• Four hands along the bottom, for passing the phone around.
• Shuffle, deal, draw, search, and fan a stack out.
• Lasso a group of cards to move, flip or gather them together.
• Snap to grid, lock cards in place, and pick a table theme.
• Undo and redo anything.
A ready-dealt game of Solitaire, with a classic 52-card deck, is included to show what tables can do.

YOURS, AND PRIVATE
Your decks live on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Share a deck or a group of decks as a single file to back it up or give it to a friend.

New to it? A short guided tour walks you through making your first deck and playing it, and Help lists every gesture.

## App Review notes
No account or sign-in. On first launch a guided tour starts (it can be skipped). A Playing Cards deck and a Solitaire table are created automatically so tables can be tried right away: open the Solitaire table from the home screen. Help (in the ☰ menu on the home screen) lists every gesture. Camera access is only used to scan cards; photo access only to import card art.

## TestFlight: What to Test
Try the guided tour (tap ? on the home screen, then Replay the Tutorial), build a deck from your own photos or a sprite sheet, and play the Solitaire table. Report anything confusing or any card that won't do what you expect.
